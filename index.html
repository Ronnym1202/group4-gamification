Help the elephant reach Graduation Hill

The Embu elephant has a long walk ahead. Every checkpoint is locked by quadratic questions, and only correct answers will open the way.

New here? Tap Practice Question first, or open the help sections below.

How to play
  • You have 3 lives in every level.
  • Each question gives you 30 seconds (unless you tick Relaxed mode).
  • A correct answer earns 10 points plus a time bonus.
  • A hint removes two wrong options but costs 5 points.
  • Get at least 3 out of 5 right to pass a level.
  • Finish all 4 levels with 70% or more to earn a Silver or Gold badge.
Tips for first-time players
  • Work the question out on paper first, then choose an option.
  • Stuck? Press Hint. It removes two wrong options and costs 5 points.
  • A wrong answer is not the end. Read the worked solution to see the steps.
  • If you fail a level you can replay it, and your lives start again at 3.
  • The practice question does not count for anything.
  • Tap Listen to hear the worked steps read aloud, or Read aloud to hear the question.
  • Use the speaker button at the top to switch sound effects on or off.
  • On a computer you can press A, B, C or D (or 1 to 4) to choose an answer.
  • Your progress is saved on this device after every question. Tap Continue to carry on where you stopped.
